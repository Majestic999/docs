## Mounting the component on the breadboard and wiring the circuit

**Step 1:** Identify all the modules and components listed in the Required Components table before starting the assembly.

**Step 2:** Connect the Sensors and Input Devices

Connect the signal pins of the sensors and input devices to the appropriate analog or digital pins on the ESP32 board, as specified in the Circuit Connections table. These connections provide the primary input signals to the ESP32 board.

**Step 3:** Connect the Actuators and Display Modules

Connect the signal pins of the actuators and display modules to their assigned PWM, digital, or I2C pins on the ESP32 board, as specified in the Circuit Connections table. These connections allow the ESP32 board to control the output devices.

**Step 4:** Connect the Power Supply

Connect the VCC or power pins of the components to the appropriate 5V or 3.3V power supply on the ESP32 board, according to the Circuit Connections table.

**Step 5:** Connect the Ground

Connect the GND pins of all components to GND on the ESP32 board.

Ensure that all components share a common ground reference.

**Step 6:** Check the Power and Ground Connections

Double-check all VCC, 5V, 3.3V, and GND connections to ensure that the polarity is correct and there are no accidental short circuits.

**Step 7:** Connect the USB Cable

After verifying all signal, power, and ground connections, connect the USB programming cable to the ESP32 board and the computer
