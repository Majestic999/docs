## Mounting the component on the breadboard and wiring the circuit

**Step 1:** Inventory all modules listed in the Required Components table.

**Step 2:** Connect the RFID Module: SS to GPIO 33, RST to GPIO 23, MOSI to 11, MISO to 12, and SCK to 13.

**Step 3:** Connect the Stepper Motor Driver (ULN2003): IN1 to GPIO 2, IN2 to GPIO 4, IN3 to GPIO 5, and IN4 to GPIO 18.

**Step 4:** Connect the 4-Digit Display (TM1637): CLK to GPIO 19 and DIO to GPIO 21.

**Step 5:** Connect line [Power Rails] to [5V / 3.3V / GND] — (System power and ground rails.).

**Step 6:** Double-check all VCC, 5V, 3.3V, and GND polarities to prevent electrical shorts.

**Step 7:** Connect USB programming cable only after verifying all signal path wiring.

![Components on breadboard](../../../assets/coder_pro/expert/expert_III/4.3.27_Project_147_RFID_Smart_Warehouse_Conveyor_Indexer/image_1.png)

_Make sure to connect the Micro USB cable to the ESP32 board._
