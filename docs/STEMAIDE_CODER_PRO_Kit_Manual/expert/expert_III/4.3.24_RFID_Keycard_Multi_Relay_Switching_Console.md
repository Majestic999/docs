- USB Cable & Jumper Wires = 1

- Bread Board


## Mounting the component on the breadboard and wiring the circuit

**Step 1:** Mount the RFID RC522 and connect SPI pins (10, 11, 12, 13) and RST (9) to the ESP32 board, powered by the 3.3V rail.

**Step 2:** Wire the IIC 1602 LCD by connecting SDA to GPIO 21 and SCL to GPIO 22, using the 5V power rail.

**Step 3:** Connect the 1-Channel Relay input pin IN1 to ESP32 board digital GPIO 2.

**Step 4:** Establish common 5V and GND rails to power the LCD and Relay modules.

**Step 5:** Double-check all VCC, 5V, 3.3V, and GND polarities to prevent electrical shorts.

**Step 6:** Connect USB programming cable only after verifying all signal path wiring.
