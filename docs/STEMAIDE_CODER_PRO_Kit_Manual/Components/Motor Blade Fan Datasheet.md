# Motor Blade Fan — Technical Datasheet

## General Description

A balanced 3-blade or 4-blade miniature plastic propeller fan designed for press-fit mounting onto the 2.0 mm cylindrical output shaft of the kit's **DC Motor 130**. Used in electromechanical demonstrators, smart cooling fan projects, and aerodynamic thrust experiments [E1].

## Specifications

| Parameter | Value | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **Propeller Type** | 3-blade or 4-blade axial flow fan | [E1] |
| **Diameter** | 50 mm to 60 mm total diameter | [E1] |
| **Bore / Shaft Diameter** | 1.95 mm interference press-fit for 2.0 mm shaft | [E1] |
| **Blade Material** | Moulded high-impact ABS / Polypropylene (PP) | [E1] |
| **Weight** | ~1.5 g (Low rotational inertia) | [E1] |
| **Maximum Safe Speed** | 15,000 RPM | [E1] |
| **Compatible Motor** | STEMAIDE DC Motor 130 | [E1] |
